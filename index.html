01TRANSFERS & RELIABILITYFaster money movement,
with correctness built in.20+ min → ~1 minP50 withdrawal latency+

I lead custody and transfer infrastructure spanning asynchronous workflows, blockchain integrations, and external vendors.

The challenge

A transfer crosses several systems. Timeouts, retries, and delayed observations can leave the outcome uncertain, even when an external action has already happened.

My contribution

Designed transaction workflows around idempotency, explicit state transitions, vendor reconciliation, and observability. Improved P50 withdrawal latency from over 20 minutes to about one minute.

The judgment call

When the outcome is ambiguous, establish what happened before repeating a money-moving action. Recovery needs to be part of the design.

02 / ENGINEERING APPROACH

The happy path
is only the beginning.

Reliable systems earn their name when something goes wrong. I think about failure modes, ownership boundaries, and recovery before a workflow reaches production.

01

Make states explicit.
Know what happened, what is pending, and what can safely happen next.

02

Design for uncertainty.
A timeout is an observation, not proof that an action failed.

03

Close the loop.
Reconcile external outcomes with internal records. Give operators a path to recovery.
